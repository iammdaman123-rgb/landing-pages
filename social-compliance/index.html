In Simple Words

What Is a Social Compliance Audit?

A social compliance audit is a check of how your factory treats its workers and runs its operations — fair pay, safe conditions, legal working hours and honest business practices.

Global buyers (brands, retailers, importers) ask for this audit before they place an order, to be sure their products are made responsibly. Pass it, and you become an approved supplier. There are a few different standards, but they all check the same four things:

01

Labour

Fair wages, legal hours, no child or forced labour.

02

Health & Safety

Safe building, fire safety, clean and healthy workplace.

03

Environment

Proper waste, water and pollution management.

04

Business Ethics

No bribery or corruption; honest record-keeping.

Standards We Handle

SEDEX / SMETA

Most requested by UK & global buyers

You register on the SEDEX platform and complete a SMETA audit (2-Pillar or 4-Pillar). The most common ask from international retailers.

  • SEDEX registration & SAQ support
  • 2-Pillar & 4-Pillar audit prep

SA8000

A full certification standard

An internationally recognised certificate for good workplace and labour practices. Valued by buyers who want a formal, audited certificate.

  • Full documentation & system setup
  • Certification-body audit support

BSCI (amfori)

Common with European buyers

The amfori BSCI audit checks your social performance against a shared code of conduct. Widely asked for by EU brands and importers.

  • Code of conduct implementation
  • Pre-audit gap check & fixes

WRAP

Standard for apparel & garment factories

WRAP certifies lawful, humane and ethical production. Frequently required for garment and sewn-product exporters, especially to the US.

  • 12-principle readiness prep
  • Also cover ICS, GOTS & GRS on request
