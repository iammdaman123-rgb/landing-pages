Understanding SEDEX

What is SEDEX & SMETA?

SEDEX (Supplier Ethical Data Exchange) is a global membership platform used by over 85,000 businesses across 180 countries to manage and improve ethical trade practices in their supply chains.

SMETA (Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit) is the audit methodology used to evaluate your factory's compliance. It is the most widely used social audit format in the world — accepted by major international buyers including Marks & Spencer, Primark, Tesco, H&M, and more.

Getting SEDEX certified signals to buyers that your factory meets international standards for worker welfare, safety, environmental responsibility, and ethical business conduct.

SMETA 2-Pillar Audit

Covers the two core areas most buyers require as a minimum.

  • Labour Standards — Fair wages, working hours, no child or forced labour, freedom of association
  • Health & Safety — Safe working conditions, fire safety, machinery safeguards, emergency procedures

SMETA 4-Pillar Audit

Comprehensive audit covering all four pillars of ethical trade compliance.

  • Labour Standards — Worker welfare, fair employment practices, and legal compliance
  • Health & Safety — Workplace safety systems, risk assessments, PPE, fire drills
  • Environment — Waste management, emissions, resource use, and environmental impact controls
  • Business Ethics — Anti-corruption, transparency, data protection, and ethical supply chain management
